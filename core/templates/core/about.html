Geek Funk Labs is a small independent workshop exploring the intersection of music, software, electronics, and science.

The projects here are built because I wanted them to exist. Some solve problems I ran into while performing music, some grew out of research or teaching, and others simply seemed like interesting ideas worth exploring. They range from Raspberry Pi musical instruments and MIDI software to astronomy tools and the occasional piece of hardware that doesn't fit neatly into any category.

Everything published by Geek Funk Labs is developed with an open-source philosophy. The source code, hardware designs, and documentation are intended to be resources to learn from—not just products to consume. If you want to build something exactly as I've designed it, that's wonderful. If you want to modify it, improve it, or use it as the starting point for something completely different, that's even better.

I sell kits and assembled hardware because not everyone wants to source components, solder boards, or troubleshoot manufacturing problems before they can start creating. Purchasing a kit helps support future development, but it is never the only way to participate. My goal is to make these projects accessible whether your contribution is code, ideas, bug reports, documentation, or simply curiosity.

About Me

I'm Bill Peterson (also known online as white2rnado), a physicist, astronomy researcher, educator, musician, and software developer.

Professionally I teach physics and astronomy while conducting research in radio astronomy. Outside the classroom I spend an unreasonable amount of time writing software, designing electronics, building synthesizers, and performing music. Many of the projects on this site exist because they turned out to be useful in one of those pursuits.

Geek Funk Labs is where those interests overlap. Rather than treating science, engineering, and art as separate disciplines, I enjoy finding places where they inform one another. A synthesizer can be an engineering project, a programming exercise, and a musical instrument all at once.
